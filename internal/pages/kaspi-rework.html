Kaspi.kz home page rework

Redesigning the main page of the largest fintech ecosystem in Kazakhstan

What kind of product?

Kaspi.kz is the #1 super app in Kazakhstan with over 14 million monthly active users.

In 2020, it became one of the fastest-growing IT-financial companies in Europe and Eurasia, as well as a key player in Kazakhstan’s financial market.Through constant innovation, Kaspi.kz drives the country’s digital transformation and strengthens its investment appeal. Its success story is even studied at Harvard.

Problem

As the business grew, investments in product development and marketing had to increase. To support this growth, the company set a clear goal — to boost product revenue.

Through user research and interviews, I identified a common challenge across products. To complete a purchase (in the *Kaspi Store* or *Kaspi Travel*), users had to navigate through multiple screens. This created a UX barrier that often led to drop-offs or users switching to alternative services. Although product and tour pages are accessible directly from the homepage, many users overlooked this feature because it was not visible within their immediate screen view. By optimizing homepage space and making purchase options more prominent, users can engage more easily, reducing friction and increasing visibility of key actions. Such improvements are expected to boost user engagement, heart encourage repeat visits, and drive growth in GMV directly from the homepage.

Goals

  • Deliver a convenient solution for product interaction while preserving the existing user experience

  • Drive growth by increasing revenue, user engagement, and retention through homepage product access

My tasks as a product designer

  • Discovery phase: define the problem and goals, establish success metrics, generate hypotheses, collect and analyze benchmarks, and design the experiment framework as a team

  • Delivery phase: create mockups and prototypes for UX research, analyze test results, refine designs into final mockups for development, and conduct design reviews after implementation.

Discovery phase

Launched an event to track user activity on the homepage and conducted surveys to uncover interaction patterns and pain points. Together with the Marketing team, defined the priority order of products to be displayed in the recommendation block.

Analyzed 50 of the most common user devices to establish the standard mobile screen resolution, and cross-referenced these findings with official technical specifications to ensure accuracy.

Based on the analysis, I selected the iPhone 11 screen resolution (375×812 px) as the reference for layout design.

Product analytics

Analysis of homepage user activity revealed the following insights:

  • GMV from the homepage totals NDA billion KZT per month

  • NDA% of users purchase in Kaspi Travel directly from the homepage

  • NDA% purchase in Store on Kaspi.kz from the homepage

  • NDA% purchase in Magnum store from the homepage

  • NDA% purchase in Store on Kaspi.kz via the recommendation block

  • NDA% purchase in Store on Kaspi.kz via the recently viewed block

  • NDA% view Store products from the recommendation block

Benchmark analysis

The analysis was based on three product types: Super Apps, E-commerce, Food-tech. Additionally, we considered: Hybrid products, Standalone product features not related to sales.

All analyzed products provide quick access to core features. Some services personalize the experience by sorting the product grid based on usage frequency. In sales-focused contexts, products and the shopping cart are immediately visible within the mobile screen frame. Notably, none of the reviewed services showcase recommendations across different verticals.

User Stories

As a store visitor, I want to see personalized product recommendations that match my interests. As a shopper, I want quick and seamless access to my cart. As a user, I want intuitive navigation to my most frequently used products

Hypotheses

Simplifying navigation to products (shopping cart, Magnum Store, Kaspi Travel, product carousel) will likely increase user interactions, leading to higher engagement and conversion rates.

Displaying recommendations within the visible screen area can encourage more users to make at least one interaction, boosting both engagement and purchase conversions.

Providing personalized recommendations may increase the number of active days users spend in the app and drive more interactions aligned with their interests.

Design experiment

We conducted an A/B test on the entire user base of Almaty (NDA million users):

Group A — control group (50% of users)

Group B — test group exposed to the redesigned homepage (50% of users)

Metrics

- Homepage → Cart conversion rate
- Homepage → Recently Viewed conversion rate
- Homepage → Featured Product conversion rate
- Products GMV comparison by group (A/B)
- Contacted users
- Active user days
- Adoption rate of new functionality
- Entry point distribution for new functionality

Delivery phase

Based on the collected data, we implemented a series of changes:
- Added a Cart button next to the search bar, reducing the transition path from two clicks to one.
- Optimized the top section of the homepage, freeing up additional space for content.
- Updated the services menu: removed Kaspi QR (already available in the tab bar) and added Magnum.
- Reduced the height of online offers, allowing more elements to fit on the screen.
- Redesigned the advertising model: replaced a single banner with a carousel, which increased GMV from the homepage.
- Added product recommendation tabs (Store, Magnum, Hot Tours), which also boosted GMV.

Solution

Based on the collected data, we implemented a series of changes:

Added a Cart button next to the search bar, reducing the transition path from two clicks to one.

Optimized the top section of the homepage, freeing up additional space for content.

Updated the services menu: removed Kaspi QR (already available in the tab bar) and added Magnum.

Reduced the height of online offers, allowing more elements to fit on the screen.

Redesigned the advertising model: replaced a single banner with a carousel, which increased GMV from the homepage.

Added product recommendation tabs (Store, Magnum, Hot Tours), which also boosted GMV.

Analysis

Our primary objective was to rapidly implement changes and drive additional revenue from the homepage. To accelerate delivery, we decided to test the solution directly in production without prior UX research.

This approach proved effective: over the course of a month, we continuously gathered user feedback and monitored performance metrics in Amplitude, validating the impact of the changes.

Based on the collected data, we observed:

- An increase in GMV of +1.2 billion KZT per month.

- Users frequently requested a Recently Viewed Products block, since they don’t always add items to the cart immediately.

- We also noticed that users rarely switch between recommendation tabs, which was confirmed by comparing click-through rates to recommended products across tabs.

Our data analysis, in collaboration with the analytics team, highlighted the need for improvements: introducing a Recently Viewed Products block and increasing the visibility of recommendation tabs to enhance user engagement.

Enhancements

After implementing the changes, we relaunched the experiment and tracked key metrics. One month later, the results demonstrated a strong impact:

- Homepage GMV increased from 1.2 billion KZT to 1.5 billion KZT per month.

- Requests for a Recently Viewed Products block stopped, while 78% of the test group actively used it.

- Recommendation tab switching frequency tripled.

- Homepage NPS rose to 88%

Summary

In conclusion, two changes stood out as the most impactful: the Recommended Products block and the Cart entry point.

- Cart adoption: one in five users across total app traffic used the cart entry point.

- Recommendation engagement: one in four engaged users interacted with the recommended products block.

Additional insights strengthened these findings:

- The Recently Viewed Products block on the homepage became a key driver of product exploration, enabling quick navigation to product detail pages.

- Within recommendations, discounted products proved most compelling — the discount tab was clicked almost twice as often as the other tabs.

These results highlight how targeted improvements to navigation and product discovery directly enhanced both engagement and user experience.
