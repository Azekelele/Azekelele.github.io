Kaspi.kz home page rework

Redesigning the main page of the largest fintech ecosystem in Kazakhstan

What kind of product?

Kaspi.kz is the #1 super app in Kazakhstan with over 14 million monthly active users.

In 2020, it became one of the fastest-growing IT-financial companies in Europe and Eurasia, as well as a key player in Kazakhstan’s financial market.Through constant innovation, Kaspi.kz drives the country’s digital transformation and strengthens its investment appeal. Its success story is even studied at Harvard.

Problem

As the business grew, investments in product development and marketing had to increase. To support this growth, the company set a clear goal — to boost product revenue.

Through user research and interviews, I identified a common challenge across products. To complete a purchase (in the *Kaspi Store* or *Kaspi Travel*), users had to navigate through multiple screens. This created a UX barrier that often led to drop-offs or users switching to alternative services. Although product and tour pages are accessible directly from the homepage, many users overlooked this feature because it was not visible within their immediate screen view. By optimizing homepage space and making purchase options more prominent, users can engage more easily, reducing friction and increasing visibility of key actions. Such improvements are expected to boost user engagement, encourage repeat visits, and drive growth in GMV directly from the homepage.
